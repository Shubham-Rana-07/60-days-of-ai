🚀 **Day 13 of #60DayClaudeChallenge | Building an AI-Powered Job Search Assistant**

Today's challenge focused on leveraging **Claude Connectors** and **Indeed integration** to build an intelligent **AI Job Search Assistant** capable of discovering relevant opportunities, evaluating job-market trends, and providing personalized career recommendations.

Traditional job searching often involves manually filtering hundreds of listings. This AI-driven workflow automates that process by analyzing a candidate's profile, matching it against current openings, identifying skill gaps, and generating actionable insights to improve employability.

### 👤 Professional Profile

* MCA Graduate
* Aspiring Data Analyst
* Technical Skills: Python, SQL, Power BI, Excel, Machine Learning, Data Visualization
* Strong interest in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, and AI-driven solutions

### 🎯 Target Job Criteria

* Data Analyst
* Junior Data Analyst
* Python Developer
* Remote, Hybrid, or On-site Opportunities
* Expected Compensation: ₹4–8 LPA

### 🤖 AI Workflow

The assistant was designed to:

• Analyze my professional profile and career objectives
• Search and filter relevant opportunities using Claude Connectors with Indeed integration
• Rank jobs based on profile compatibility and relevance
• Identify the most in-demand technical skills across current openings
• Perform a comprehensive skill gap analysis
• Provide market demand insights and personalized recommendations to strengthen my profile and interview readiness
