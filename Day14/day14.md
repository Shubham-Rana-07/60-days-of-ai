# AI Job Red Flag Detector

An AI-powered tool that analyzes job descriptions to identify potential red flags, misleading job titles, unrealistic expectations, and other hiring risks.

## Features
-  Job Title vs. Role Analysis
-  Red Flag Detection
-  Overall Risk Score
-  Positive Signal Identification
-  Risk Breakdown by Category
-  Smart Interview Questions
-  Final Hiring Verdict

## Use Cases
- Evaluate job descriptions before applying
- Detect misleading or poorly written JDs
- Prepare smarter interview questions
- Make informed career decisions

## Tech Stack
- Claude AI
- Prompt Engineering
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Markdown

## Output
- Overall Risk Score
- Top Red Flags
- Positive Signals
- Risk Breakdown
- Final Verdict
- Recommended Interview Questions
