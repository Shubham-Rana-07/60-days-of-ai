- Average Entry-Level Salary

- Career Growth Potential
- Work-Life Balance
- Remote Work Availability

## Primary Sources

- AmbitionBox

- LinkedIn Jobs
- Government Labour Statistics (where applicable)

## Notes

- All factual values are taken from publicly available sources.
- Estimated values are clearly marked within the application.
- Multiple sources were cross-checked where possible.
