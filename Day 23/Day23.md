| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Startup | IndustryBridge |
| Market | India |
| Stage | Pre-Seed |
| Core Problem | Students lack practical industry experience |
| Initial Segment | College Students |

| Business Model | Freemium + B2B |
| Validation Status | Promising but Unvalidated |

IndustryBridge is an EdTech platform designed to bridge the gap between **academic education and real-world industry experience**.

The platform connects college students with startups, companies, and industry professionals through structured internship programs where students can work on **real projects, develop professional skills, receive mentorship, and gain practical exposure**.

---

# Problem Statement

Many college students graduate with strong theoretical knowledge but limited practical experience.

Students often face difficulties such as:

- Lack of real-world project experience
- Difficulty finding genuine internships
- Limited exposure to professional work environments
- Lack of industry mentorship
- Skill gaps between academics and job requirements
- Difficulty understanding how companies actually operate
- Internship opportunities that provide certificates but limited practical learning

This creates a significant gap between **college education and industry expectations**.

---

# Solution

IndustryBridge aims to create an ecosystem where students can gain practical industry experience before graduation.

The platform will provide:

- Structured internship programs
- Real-world industry projects
- Industry mentors
- Skill development
- Team-based projects
- Performance evaluation
- Internship certificates
- Portfolio-ready project experience
- Career and placement guidance

The goal is to help students become **job-ready through practical experience rather than theoretical learning alone**.

# Final Verdict

## Promising but Unvalidated

### Strengths

- Large student market
- Clear education-to-employment gap
- Strong demand for practical experience
- Multiple potential revenue streams
- Opportunity to build a long-term student talent ecosystem

### Challenges

- Competition from established platforms
- Maintaining internship quality
- Building company partnerships
- Student acquisition and retention
- Ensuring internships provide genuine practical experience

### Recommendation

Proceed with a **focused MVP targeting college students and startups/SMEs**.

The initial goal should not be to build a massive internship marketplace.

Instead, start with:

**25 Students + 5 Companies + Real Projects + Mentorship**

and measure whether students actually gain meaningful industry experience.

---

# Key Learnings

- Students don't just need internship certificates; they need **real industry experience**.
- Start with one clear customer segment.
- Validate student and employer problems before building the platform.
- Company partnerships are critical for creating genuine opportunities.
- Project-based internships can create stronger outcomes than simple internship listings.
- A focused MVP can validate the business model before large-scale development.
