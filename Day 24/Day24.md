# Startup in Brief

IndustryBridge is a curated internship-matching platform designed to help Indian college students gain **real industrial experience** through verified internships, practical projects, and direct interaction with startups and SMEs.

The initial strategy is to start with a focused **Tech/Data internship vertical** instead of trying to serve students across every industry.

### Problem

Students often struggle with:

* Lack of practical industry experience
* Unverified internship listings
* Unresponsive employers
* Limited access to real-world projects
* Lack of mentorship and professional exposure

### Target Customers

**Students:** Final-year college students interested in Tech/Data careers.

**Employers:** Local startups and SMEs looking for affordable, motivated interns.

### Initial MVP

The first version will be intentionally manual:

* WhatsApp-based applications
* Google Forms for student registration
* Manually sourced internships
* Spreadsheet-based tracking
* Direct student-employer matching
* 48-hour response commitment

The objective is to validate demand before building a full marketplace.

# Competitive Positioning

Existing platforms such as Internshala, LinkedIn and Unstop provide access to a large number of opportunities.

IndustryBridge will initially compete through **focus rather than scale**.

### Differentiation

**Verified Opportunities + Fast Employer Response + Practical Experience + Student Support**

Instead of simply listing internships, the goal is to help students successfully move from:

**Application → Selection → Project → Experience → Career Opportunity**

---

# MVP Strategy

The first version will not require a complex website or mobile application.

### Manual MVP Workflow

```text
Student
   ↓
Google Form
   ↓
Student Screening
   ↓
Verified Internship Matching
   ↓
Employer Confirmation
   ↓
WhatsApp Communication
   ↓
Internship / Project
   ↓
Feedback & Outcome Tracking
```

This approach allows the startup to test the business model with minimal cost.

---

# 48-Hour Response Promise

One of the initial value propositions is a **48-hour guaranteed response**.

Students should know whether:

* Their application has been received
* Their profile is being reviewed
* They have been shortlisted
* The employer has declined the application
* Additional information is required

The purpose is to solve one of the common frustrations students face on existing internship platforms: **applying but receiving no meaningful response**.

# Key Risks

# Sustainability Strategy

IndustryBridge should avoid heavy technology investment before validating the core business model.

### Phase 1 — Manual Validation

WhatsApp + Google Forms + Spreadsheet

### Phase 2 — MVP Platform

Student profiles + internship listings + applications + employer dashboard

### Phase 3 — Automation

Automated matching + notifications + analytics + mentor systems

### Phase 4 — Scale

College partnerships + employer network + multiple career verticals

This staged approach reduces financial risk and allows the product to grow according to validated demand.

---

# Validation Framework

The most important questions to answer during the pilot are:

1. Will students actively apply through IndustryBridge?
2. Can we consistently find genuine internship opportunities?
3. Will companies pay for successful student placements?
4. Does the 48-hour response promise improve student satisfaction?
5. Can we achieve successful placements manually?
6. Do students complete the internships?
7. Are employers satisfied with the student talent?
8. Will employers return for more interns?
9. Which acquisition channel produces the best students?
10. Can this process eventually be automated profitably?

# Business Model Hypothesis

```text
Students
   ↓
Free Registration
   ↓
Internship Matching
   ↓
Verified Company
   ↓
Successful Placement
   ↓
Employer Pays Placement Fee
```

The initial business model is **B2B2C**, where students receive free access while companies pay for successful talent acquisition.

---

# Key Learning

The biggest lesson from today's strategy work is:

> **Do not build a large platform before proving that the basic matching process works.**

IndustryBridge can begin with a simple manual system, validate the demand, understand student and employer behaviour, and only then invest in software development.
