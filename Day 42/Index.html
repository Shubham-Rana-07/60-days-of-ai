Money Tips for Students

✅
Follow the 50/30/20 rule (adapted)Even on pocket money, split into needs, wants, and savings — try 60% needs, 20% wants, 20% savings.
✅
Track every rupee for a weekSmall daily spends (tea, snacks, autos) add up fast — awareness is the first step to control.
✅
Use student discountsAlways ask — many apps, transport, and software offer verified student pricing.
⚠️
Watch subscription creepMultiple small subscriptions (OTT, music, cloud storage) silently drain limited allowance.
