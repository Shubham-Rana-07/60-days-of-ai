What is Interview Signal?

This is a personal toolkit for turning your job search writing into repeatable AI systems, instead of retyping similar prompts every time. It's built around what you told us: you're a data analyst on the job hunt, mainly writing application emails and cover letters, and you want a clear system rather than guesswork.

  • Workflows — ready-to-edit prompt templates for your job search tasks (cover letters, follow-ups, LinkedIn messages, interview prep). Fill in a few blanks and copy the result.
  • Prompt Builder — assemble a custom prompt piece by piece (role, goal, context, tone, etc.) with a live preview, so you learn the anatomy of a good prompt instead of memorizing one.
  • Loop Builder — turns any one-shot prompt into a prompt that tells the AI to keep improving its own answer until it meets a bar you set.

What's a workflow?

A workflow is a reusable prompt template with blanks (called variables) you fill in — like [Company Name] or [Job Title]. Instead of writing a new prompt from scratch every time you apply somewhere, you reuse the same proven structure and swap the details.

What is the Prompt Builder?

A good prompt is made of small, swappable pieces — not one giant block of text. Add the pieces you need from the left, pick a preset or write your own for each, and watch the final prompt assemble itself on the right. This teaches the underlying system so you can eventually build prompts without this tool.

What is the Loop Builder?

A normal prompt gives you one answer. A looping prompt instructs the AI to check its own answer against criteria you set, improve it, and repeat — until it hits a stop condition (like "3 rounds" or "meets all criteria"). Useful for things like polishing a cover letter draft until it's genuinely strong, not just first-pass output.
