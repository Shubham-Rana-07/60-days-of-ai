Every line on your resume is a claim.
This is where it gets tested.

Not a resume reviewer. Not a grammar check. This is an adversarial panelist that reads your experience, extracts every claim you're making — quantified, vague, or implied — and cross-examines you on each one, the way a sharp technical interviewer would.

Answers get scored for specificity. Weak spots get flagged. By the end you get a Defense Report showing exactly what's ready and what will fall apart under pressure.
