# Day 50 – Defend Your Experience

## Project
Defend Your Experience – AI-powered Adaptive Interview Defense Simulator

## Objective
Build an AI system that cross-examines every claim on a resume and tests whether the candidate can defend it under interview pressure.

## Features

- Resume upload
- AI claim extraction
- Adaptive cross-questioning
- Technical interview simulation
- Defense readiness score
- Weakness identification
- Interview feedback
- Final defense report

## What I Learned

- Importance of defending every resume claim
- Difference between portfolio projects and production projects
- How interviewers test ownership and depth
- Importance of measurable impact and business outcomes

## Outcome

Successfully completed an adaptive AI interview simulation and identified weak resume areas before real interviews.
